**Hear your agents.**

Coding agents finish in silence. Echo speaks the completion line when the turn ends, and supported hosts also announce when they are waiting on you. One local daemon on your Mac at `localhost:3246`. Any host that can POST JSON.

Echo speaks, and it listens. Speech-to-text ships today as voice ask: an agent asks you a question out loud and gets your spoken answer back as text, transcribed on your Mac.

Echo has two separate voice features:
- **[Notifications (text-to-speech)](/docs/notifications/)**: Echo talks to you.
- **[Voice ask (speech-to-text)](/docs/speech-to-text/)**: an agent asks you one question out loud, and your spoken answer comes back as text.
